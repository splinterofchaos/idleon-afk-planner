Update from game data

Don't want to paste every time? Run Start Idleon Planner on your PC. That app reads your save by itself every minute while its window is open.

  1. Open Idleon Toolbox and sign in.
  2. Open your profile menu at the top right and choose Copy for support. This copy includes your character names.
  3. Come back here and press Ctrl+V. You can paste anywhere on this page.

This fills in each character's class, map, current activity, skill levels, EXP and the time they went AFK. It keeps only those values and doesn't store the rest of your save. The game can't report your rates, so kills and EXP per hour stay as you typed them. The data is as fresh as your last cloud save.

These rows are examples. Replace them with your own characters.

Numbers can be 450k, 1.2m, 2.5b, 3t or 4q. Enter and the arrow keys move up and down a column. Each character shows what the game says it's doing, and remembers your rates and goals for when it comes back to that activity. Goals are optional. Skill and class goals can be a level (250) or a gain (+5). Rare drop times are averages, using your kills per hour, the drop's listed chance and the drop rate you enter, so type the drop rate your character shows in game. Multikill, from the game's AFK Info screen, counts each kill several times: drops on the monster's own list come that many more times, drops from a table beneath it don't. Level costs use the game's formulas, as published by Idleon Toolbox. Spelunking's isn't published, so its formula was worked out from the level costs stored in real saves. The wait assumes your EXP per hour stays the same as you level.
